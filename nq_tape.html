How this tape was built

—
Market fills one tick through the last revealed price. Stops and targets are checked on each one-second bar after the fill. A stop that gaps fills at the open, then one more tick. If stop and target are both inside the same second, the one closer to the open is used, and a tie is the stop. Charting is TradingView Lightweight Charts with drawing tools in this page. Click the pointer or press Escape to leave a tool. Click a drawing to select it, drag to move, drag a handle to resize, and press Delete to remove it.
